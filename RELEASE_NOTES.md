Free games. Setup → Free games lists about 1,300 games their makers give away, by console: homebrew from Homebrew Hub (Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, NES) and libretro's freeware (WASM-4, Vectrex, Mega Drive, a few for other consoles, and the shareware episode of Doom). A shows what a game is; Get it downloads it into the right ROM folder with its name, description and cover, and when you leave, Arcadia rescans and shows that console. With no games yet, the welcome screen and Setup's first step lead there, so a fresh install has something to play in a minute. Dialogs now show over the screen they came from (dimmed) instead of over a blank background.
