# Console pictures

Every picture in this folder is original to Arcadia. Each console is modelled as a small 3D drawing sized in real
centimetres (`tools/console_models.py`), with its details placed from photographs of the real hardware used as
reference only, then drawn straight at the pixel size and colour depth of its generation (`tools/isoart.py`,
`tools/make_console_art.py`): about 56 pixels wide and 12 colours for an 8-bit console, up to a few times that for
the HD era. No photograph is copied or included. The collection tiles and the generic cartridge come from
`tools/make_extra_art.py`.

Console names and designs belong to their makers; the pictures are only there to show which console is which.
